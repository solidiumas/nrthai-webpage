02 · How we work

Map. Pilot. Run.

  1. 1

    Step 1, Map · 1 week

    We sit with your team, watch the work happen, and find the 3–5 places where AI changes the numbers.

  2. 2

    Step 2, Pilot · 2–3 weeks

    One solution in production on a bounded part of the work. Real data, real users, measurable results.

  3. 3

    Step 3, Run · ongoing

    We monitor, improve and expand. With logs, control, and a human in the loop where it matters.

The math

A skilled employee in Norway costs NOK 1–1.5 million a year. When AI supports your people and takes the routine work, your team can gain the capacity of one or more extra employees, and quality goes up. AI typically costs 20–40% of what one employee costs.

Employee per year NOK 1–1.5M
Cost of AI 20–40%
