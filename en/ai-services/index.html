✦ AI services

Solutions that sell more and save time.

We build AI solutions around your processes and data. From a single AI employee that takes routine work, to custom systems and AI that runs on your own hardware.

Book a meeting →
01

AI employees

An AI employee takes on a specific role or workflow: document processing, customer service, analysis, internal knowledge or sales follow-up. Around the clock, at a fraction of the cost of a hire.

Included

  • → Mapping of the role or workflow
  • → An AI employee built around your processes
  • → Integration with your existing tools and data
  • → Deployment on local infrastructure when your data requires it
  • → Ongoing improvement and monitoring
Delivery
1–3 weeks
Investment
Setup from about NOK 50,000, operations from NOK 5,000 per month. Typically 15–35% of the cost of a hire.
Best fit
Companies that want more capacity without hiring, especially in roles with repetitive knowledge work or high volumes.
02

Custom solutions

When off-the-shelf tools don't fit, we design and build the solution that does. From first idea to solution in production.

Included

  • → Discovery and scoping
  • → Architecture and solution design
  • → Development, adaptation and integration
  • → Testing, deployment and documentation
  • → Further development when needed
Delivery
4–16 weeks
Investment
Fixed price per project. Small from about NOK 150,000, mid-size from NOK 400,000, large from NOK 1,000,000.
Best fit
Companies with a specific problem where existing tools fall short on data sensitivity, integration or domain.
03

Local AI infrastructure

AI that runs on your own hardware, inside your network. We choose the hardware, set up the models and secure the solution.

Included

  • → Hardware specification and purchasing support
  • → Language model setup, open-source or licensed
  • → Adaptation to your data
  • → Security architecture and access control
  • → Documentation, handover and training
Delivery
2–4 weeks
Investment
Typically NOK 100,000 to 1,000,000+, depending on scale.
Best fit
Regulated industries and companies that treat control over their own data as a strategic choice, not a box to tick.
