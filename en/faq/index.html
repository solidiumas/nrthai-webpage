What businesses ask us most often.

What does Nrth AI do?

We help businesses adopt AI: we assess where AI does the most, implement it, and build AI services such as AI assistants and custom solutions.

How do we get started?

With a meeting. Book at nrth.no/en/book. Then we follow three steps: map (1 week), pilot (2–3 weeks) and run.

What does it cost?

AI assessment from NOK 35,000. AI assistants from about NOK 50,000 in setup and NOK 5,000 per month in operations. Custom solutions from about NOK 150,000. All prices are on nrth.no.

Do you give talks and courses?

Yes, for leadership teams, staff and conferences. See nrth.no/en/talks-and-courses.

Where are you based?

In Bergen, Norway. We meet businesses in person across Norway, and online with clients abroad.
